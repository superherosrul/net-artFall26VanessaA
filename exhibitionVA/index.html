Misrepresentation

Net Art Exhbition Curated by Vanessa Amfo-Ayeh

Misrepresentation is an online museum that represents web art pieces from inspiring artists. This will showcase both digital and physical artwork. This also will include info on the innovation that these artists are creating in the technological world. These artists are continuing to trailblaze and set a new bar for creativity and expression in the digital and physical world. Showing how physical and digital reality can blend together is a difficult task that I believe Mendi Lewis Obadike, IFEOMA, and Chelsea Jones nailed in the early 2000s and continue to do so. If you pay attention to the digital art pieces you’ll find that the message conveyed will tell a real story.

Keeping Up Appearances By Mendi Lewis Obadike 2001
This piece represents how a person may omit pieces of a story to make it sound lighter. However, the truth is important especially when it comes to making sure your voice is heard.


IFEOMA By IFEOMA 2008
IFEOMA is her name and she creates physical and digital art pieces. She created this website to express who she is and y what she now does for a living. IFEOMA left her job in healthcare to pursue her artistry and now brings her idea to life with physical and digital media.


Return to Eden By Chelsea Jones 2020
Chelsea Jones creates physical and digital art pieces. She also uses AI for her artwork but in a way that I wouldn’t have thought of. She uses AI to generate different parts of art to help Jones plan how to piece together her portrait. She uses these AI generated pieces and makes her own variations to paint her work. She also uses an AI robot called Artmatr that will take digital artwork or an AI generated art piece and paint that image onto a physical canvas using acrylic or oil paint.
